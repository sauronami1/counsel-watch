How this board works

Only people with the password can read this board: the listings are encrypted, and the page decrypts them on your device. Every morning a search runs across the Ontario Public Service, Ontario regulators and agencies, municipalities, federal bodies with Toronto offices, telecom carriers, banks and tech companies, legal job boards, and Job Bank. Each role is only added after its posting page is opened and confirmed open, in the right place, and posted in the last 30 days. Roles are re-checked daily and drop off when they close.

Strong fit means the work itself is policy, regulatory, telecom or privacy law. Other roles touch those areas but are partly commercial or general counsel work. Roles marked Related role are not lawyer positions but are policy, privacy or regulatory jobs where a law degree is a strong fit. Saved, Applied and Not for me sort roles into their own tabs; notes stay with each role.
